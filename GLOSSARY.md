# Bowling

Ubiquitous language for Ten-Pin Bowling game tracking and scoring.

## Language

**Game**:
A complete sequence of ten frames played by a single bowler.
_Avoid_: Match, round, session

**Frame**:
A single unit of play within a game consisting of up to two rolls, or up to three in the tenth frame.
_Avoid_: Round, turn, stage

**Roll**:
A single delivery of the bowling ball down the lane aimed at knocking down pins.
_Avoid_: Shot, throw, attempt

**Pin**:
One of the ten wooden targets arranged in an equilateral triangle at the end of the lane.
_Avoid_: Bottle, skittle, stick

**Strike**:
The knockdown of all ten pins on the first roll of a frame.
_Avoid_: Pleno, perfect roll

**Spare**:
The knockdown of all remaining pins on the second roll of a frame where pins were left standing after the first roll.
_Avoid_: Semipleno, cover, pick-up

**Open Frame**:
A frame in which neither a strike nor a spare is achieved, leaving pins standing after all allowed rolls.
_Avoid_: Missed frame, error, failed frame

**Score**:
The cumulative total of points earned across frames, factoring in pin counts and bonus rolls from strikes and spares.
_Avoid_: Tally, points, marks

**Tenth Frame**:
The final frame of a game which permits a third roll if a strike or spare is scored in the first two rolls.
_Avoid_: Final round, bonus frame

**Bonus Roll**:
An additional roll earned in the tenth frame as a result of achieving a strike or spare.
_Avoid_: Extra shot, fill ball, free roll

**Notation**:
The symbolic way of recording a roll on a scoresheet: `X` for a strike, `/` for a spare, `-` for zero pins, digits otherwise.
_Avoid_: Shorthand, symbols, codes

## Online play

**Bowler**:
A person taking part in a Room under a nickname, with a Game of their own.
_Avoid_: Player, user, participant

**Room**:
An online space where up to six Bowlers play their Games together, from Lobby to final Score, and that disappears afterwards.
_Avoid_: Match, session, lobby, table

**Room Code**:
The five-character code that identifies a Room while it exists.
_Avoid_: Room ID, PIN, password

**Invite Link**:
The link that opens a Room directly from its Room Code.
_Avoid_: Share URL, invitation

**Host**:
The Bowler who starts the Room's Games and may remove other Bowlers.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin, leader

**Lobby**:
The phase of a Room before its Games start, the only time Bowlers can join.
_Avoid_: Waiting room, pre-game

**Frame Window**:
The shared time limit in which every Bowler in a Room plays the same Frame; Rolls not made when it closes count as zero pins.
_Avoid_: Turn, round, timer

**Rematch**:
A fresh set of Games in the same Room for the Bowlers still present.
_Avoid_: Replay, new game, restart

**Swipe**:
The finger gesture on the Lane that makes a Roll, read as release position, direction, speed and spin.
_Avoid_: Throw, flick, drag

**Lane**:
The surface on screen where the ball travels and the Pins stand.
_Avoid_: Alley, track, field
