# Bowling

Ubiquitous language for Ten-Pin Bowling game tracking and scoring.

## Language

**Game**:
A complete sequence of ten frames played by a single bowler.
_Avoid_: Match, round, session

**Frame**:
A single unit of play within a game consisting of up to two rolls, or up to three in the tenth frame.
_Avoid_: Round, turn, stage

**Roll**:
A single delivery of the bowling ball down the lane aimed at knocking down pins.
_Avoid_: Shot, throw, attempt

**Pin**:
One of the ten wooden targets arranged in an equilateral triangle at the end of the lane.
_Avoid_: Bottle, skittle, stick

**Strike**:
The knockdown of all ten pins on the first roll of a frame.
_Avoid_: Pleno, perfect roll

**Spare**:
The knockdown of all remaining pins on the second roll of a frame where pins were left standing after the first roll.
_Avoid_: Semipleno, cover, pick-up

**Open Frame**:
A frame in which neither a strike nor a spare is achieved, leaving pins standing after all allowed rolls.
_Avoid_: Missed frame, error, failed frame

**Score**:
The cumulative total of points earned across frames, factoring in pin counts and bonus rolls from strikes and spares.
_Avoid_: Tally, points, marks

**Tenth Frame**:
The final frame of a game which permits a third roll if a strike or spare is scored in the first two rolls.
_Avoid_: Final round, bonus frame

**Bonus Roll**:
An additional roll earned in the tenth frame as a result of achieving a strike or spare.
_Avoid_: Extra shot, fill ball, free roll
